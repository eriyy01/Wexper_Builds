First message to live site

How we work

Four steps, in order, from your first message to a site that is live and yours. Nothing between them is a surprise: the price is agreed before we start, and the delivery date is confirmed in writing.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    Every project starts with a message, not a meeting: through the form, on WhatsApp, or by email, whichever you reach for. Tell us what your business does, roughly which pages you need, and when you would like it live. If you already have a logo, photos or copy, send those too. If you do not, say so, because we can work without them.

    You will hear back from us within one working day, with any questions we have about the job and a written quote.

    • What your business does
    • The pages you have in mind
    • Anything you already have: logo, photos, copy
    • When you want it live

  2. 02

    Approve a fixed price

    The quote sets out the package, what is included, the payment schedule, the delivery date we are working to, and whether GST applies. It is one price for the whole build. The ₹7,999 and ₹14,999 prices on this site are what you pay, with no retainer or monthly fee behind them.

    Work begins only once you have approved it. If the scope changes later, we agree that in writing before anything extra is built.

    • One price for the build, agreed up front
    • Payment schedule and GST set out in the quote
    • Delivery date confirmed in writing
    • No retainer, no monthly fee

  3. 03

    We design and build

    We start with how the site looks and reads, because that is the part worth settling early. Once you are happy with the design, we build it into the real thing: fast, responsive, and working on a phone as well as a desktop.

    You review as we go rather than seeing everything at the end. Your quote sets out which changes are covered; on the Managed package, revisions continue for as long as your site is under our management.

    • Design first, so you can react to it
    • Reviews at points agreed before we start
    • Change requests answered in plain language
    • Unlimited revisions while under management (Managed)

  4. 04

    Launch and hand over

    We register the domain, set up cloud hosting and take the site live. Then you get everything: the code, the assets, the domain registered in your name and the hosting account. Nothing is locked behind our platform.

    From the second year, renewals are charged at whatever the registrar and host charge. On Starter you run the site yourself or move it anywhere you like; on Managed, the renewals and the site stay with us.

    • Domain registered in your name
    • Cloud hosting set up for the first year
    • Full code and asset handoff
    • Renewals at cost from year two
