What it should be doing for you.

Not upgrades, not add-ons, not a second invoice later. Both packages are built the same way; the difference is what happens after launch. This is what the site is for.

Being found

They search for you before they ring you.

A job is often decided in the search results, before you ever hear about it. If what comes up is a dead page or the place two streets over, that is who gets the call. The search work is finished before you launch: clean structure, real headings, and answers marked up so search engines can show them.

On their phone

They are looking you up one-handed.

Most people checking you out are holding a phone, often while they are doing something else. That is where the design starts. One site, checked across phones, tablets, laptops and wide monitors, with nothing to pinch, zoom or squint at.

Enquiries

A WhatsApp button and an inbox that both work.

People message when it suits them, which is usually when your hands are full. Every site comes with an enquiry form and a WhatsApp button wired to your phone, so the message sent at 11pm is one you read at 8am, not one lost under an old post.

Credibility

Small team. Proper presence.

Before anyone rings a small business they check it is still trading. A fast site on your own domain, with real prices and real answers, settles that in the few seconds they spend looking, so the first message is about the job, not ‘are you still going?’

Your time

The five questions you keep answering.

What does it cost? How long does it take? Can you do X? Put them on the page, with the FAQ marked up so search engines show the answers too. The repeat calls stop, and what lands in your inbox is worth reading.

Speed

They will not wait for it to load.

A slow page says nothing at all, because they are back on the search results before it finishes. Every build ships light: properly sized images, no heavy plugins, and pages that appear on an ordinary connection.

Questions, answered.

Everything people ask before they hire us. If yours is missing, the contact form just below reaches us directly.

How much does a website cost?

The Starter package is ₹7,999 one-time and the Managed package is ₹14,999 one-time. Both include a custom design, a domain name and cloud hosting for the first year. There is no monthly fee and no retainer.

What is the difference between Starter and Managed?

Starter is the build itself: design, domain, hosting, one full round of customisation before launch, and a complete handoff of the code and assets, after which the site is yours to run. Managed is everything in Starter plus as many rounds of customisation as you need, unlimited revisions while your site is under our management, renewals handled for you, full site management, and a priority support response.

Will my site work on a phone as well as a computer?

Yes. That is not an extra, it is how we build. One site, checked across phones, tablets, laptops and wide screens before it goes live. Nothing to pinch or zoom, and the phone layout is designed first, because that is where most people arrive.

Is SEO included, and will I rank on Google?

SEO is built into every site: clean structure, proper headings, fast pages, an FAQ section marked up so search engines can read it, and the technical basics settled before launch. What nobody honest can promise is a position on page one. That depends on your market, your content and time. We make sure nothing on our side is holding you back.

Can I have dark mode, and can you embed Google Maps?

Both, at no extra cost. Dark mode can ship as a light theme, a dark theme, or both, following what the visitor’s device is already set to. You choose before we build. Google Maps is embedded where people look for it, on the contact page and in the footer, with directions one tap away; send us your pin and it is live on launch day.

What if I want changes after it goes live?

Starter includes one full round of customisation before launch: you review it, tell us what to change, and we change it. After that, extra changes are quoted individually, or handled under Managed, which covers multiple rounds of customisation and unlimited revisions for as long as your site is under our management.

Do I own the website?

Yes. On the Starter package you receive the full code and asset handoff, along with the domain registered in your name and the hosting account. Nothing is locked behind our platform.

Is domain and hosting really included?

For the first year, yes. We register the domain and set up cloud hosting as part of the package. From year two, renewals are charged at whatever the registrar and host charge. You can pay them directly, or we can keep paying them for you under the Managed package.

What do you need from me to start?

Send us a message through the contact form, on WhatsApp, or by email, with what you need and when you want it live. We reply within one working day, confirm scope and price, and take it from there. If you already have a logo, copy or photos, send those too. If not, we can work without them.

How long does a build take?

It depends on how much content you have ready and how quickly feedback comes back. We confirm a delivery date in writing before work begins, so you are never guessing.

How do I pay?

Every project begins with a written quote, and no work starts until you have approved it. The payment schedule, the methods we accept and whether GST is included are all set out in that quote before you commit, so you know the full picture before anything begins.

What if I don’t like the design?

Scope and price are agreed before any work begins, and your written quote sets out what changes are covered along the way. The Starter package includes one full round of customisation before launch; on the Managed package, revisions continue for as long as your site is under our management.

Who are you, and where are you based?

Wexper Builds is a small web studio run by the people who do the work. No account managers, no outsourcing between your first message and your launch. We reply to every enquiry within one working day.

What happens after the first year?

Domain and hosting renewals are billed at the registrar’s and host’s cost each year, whether you pay them yourself or we handle them for you under the Managed package. Nothing about the site itself stops working. It is your code on your accounts.
