Privacy policy

Wexper Builds is a small web studio. This page explains in plain language what happens to the information you give us, and what does not happen to it.

What we collect

The only form on this site is the contact form, and it asks for your name, your email address and a description of your project. Nothing else is collected about you while you browse.

We do not run advertising, we do not sell data, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

How we use it

Your details are used for one thing: replying to you. If you hire us, the same information becomes part of our project records so we can keep answering your questions later.

We never add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Form delivery

Contact form messages are passed to us through Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. They process the message on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes.

If you would rather not use the form, email wexperbuild@gmail.com directly.

Hosting and logs

This site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare records basic request information, such as IP address, browser and the page requested, in server logs for security and reliability purposes. We do not have access to a profile of you from those logs, and we do not use them for marketing.

Cookies and local storage

There are no advertising or tracking cookies on this site. The only thing we keep in your browser is your light or dark theme choice, stored locally so the site remembers it next time. Clearing your browser data removes it.

How long we keep it

Enquiries we do not work on are deleted once it is clear you are not interested. Project records are kept for as long as we need them to deliver and support the work, and for the tax and legal periods that apply to a small business in India.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email wexperbuild@gmail.com and we will action it. No form, no fee.

Changes to this page

If the way this site handles data changes, this page changes with it. The date at the top tells you when it last happened.

Questions about this page go to wexperbuild@gmail.com.
